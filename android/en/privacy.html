Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Birder Diary ("we", "our", or "the app") is a bird-watching diary app. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the app and its related services.

1. Information We Process

a) Phone Number Sign-In

To create or access an account, you provide a mobile phone number and enter a verification code sent to that number. We use the necessary identity and account information to verify you, protect the account, and keep you signed in. The login page requires you to actively agree to the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy before continuing.

b) Diary, Media, and Profile Information

Your diary records, photos, videos, notes, and profile information are stored locally on your device. Optional location information that you enter or that is contained in media you select is also kept with your local diary.

c) Bird Identification

When you actively request bird identification, we process only the content needed to provide the result and protect the service. Content unrelated to your request is not processed for identification.

2. How We Use Information

  • Verify your identity, maintain sessions, and protect your account
  • Provide bird identification when you request it
  • Organize and display your diary and media on your device
  • Prevent abuse and keep the service reliable
  • Respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests

3. Storage and Account Deletion

Your diary and media data are stored locally on your device. We store only the identity and account information needed to provide and protect your account on our servers.

When you confirm account deletion, we remove the phone-number identity binding, deactivate the account, and revoke its sessions. After the service confirms the request, the app clears its local diary and media data. Information that must be retained by law may remain for the required period.

4. Service Providers and Information Sharing

Some functions rely on contracted service providers. We provide them only the information needed for the relevant function and require them to protect it. We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for third-party advertising or marketing profiles.

5. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Birder Diary does not use your information for advertising, cross-app tracking, or marketing profiles.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You may choose not to select media or request bird identification. You may edit or delete local diary content, sign out, or request account deletion in the app. Depending on applicable law, you may also request access to, correction of, a copy of, or deletion of personal information, or withdraw consent where applicable, by contacting us.

7. Children's Privacy

Birder Diary is not directed at children under 14 in mainland China or below the minimum age required by applicable law elsewhere. We do not knowingly process children's personal information in violation of applicable law.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app and service change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at feedback@birderdiary.com.
